# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We want this project to be a respectful, practical, and harassment-free place for contributors and users.

## Expected Behavior

- Be constructive and specific.
- Respect different experience levels and viewpoints.
- Protect other people's credentials, identifiers, and message content.
- Accept maintainers' decisions about safety, scope, and Meta platform compliance.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Sharing credentials, private webhook data, or Instagram user information.
- Deliberately encouraging scraping, private endpoints, spam, or platform abuse.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues, pull requests, or contributions that violate this policy. Repeated or serious violations may result in a temporary or permanent ban from project spaces.

Report sensitive conduct concerns privately through [GitHub private reporting](https://github.com/dangogit/instagram-cta-kit/security/advisories/new). Do not include another person's private data unless it is necessary to understand the report.

This policy applies in repository spaces and when someone is publicly representing the project.
